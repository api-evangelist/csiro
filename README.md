CSIRO, the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation, is Australia's national science agency and innovation catalyst, an Australian Government body that solves major challenges through science and technology across areas such as agriculture, health, oceans and climate, energy, digital and space. Its Data Access Portal (DAP) publishes RESTful web service APIs: a public read-only /dap/ws/v2 collections API for searching and retrieving research data collections, and a staff-only /dap/api/v2 deposit API. CSIRO was surfaced via the australia-apidays-2026 harvest.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for CSIRO: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
